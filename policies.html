2. Returns & Refunds Architecture

Quality validation guarantees protecting your investment

Strict Factory Default Enforcements

To maintain our quality standards and eradicate substandard gray-market processing components, returns and refunds are authorized exclusively for documented factory-defaulted hardware errors.

Covered Conditions:

  • Internal motherboard faults or dead components.
  • Unprovoked display driver pixel drops upon unboxing.
  • Factory firmware configuration failure scripts.

Excluded Conditions:

  • Physical falls, continuous surface scuffs, or liquid ingestion.
  • Software modifications or custom kernel flashing failures.
  • Change-of-mind instances after pristine physical seal delivery.
